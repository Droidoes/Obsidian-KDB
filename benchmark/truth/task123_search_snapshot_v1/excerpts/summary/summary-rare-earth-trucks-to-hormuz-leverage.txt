
This document summarizes a May 2026 post-summit strategic analysis examining the geopolitical leverage between the US and China following the Trump-Xi summit in Beijing. It details the physical dependencies of [[rare-earth-supply-chains]] running through Myanmar, the non-viability of the [[myanmar-side-door]] supply route through India, and how US naval actions in the [[strait-of-hormuz]] during [[operation-epic-fury]] are constrained by Chinese threats of rare-earth retaliation. The broader strategic balance is synthesized in [[us-china-asymmetric-leverage-dynamic]].
