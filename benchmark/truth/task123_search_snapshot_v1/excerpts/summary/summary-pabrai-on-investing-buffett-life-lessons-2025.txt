
In this video interview with Sonia Shenoy, investor Mohnish Pabrai shares core principles from his [[value-investing]] methodology, personal experiences with Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger, and his broader life philosophy. Pabrai outlines the crucial role of the [[inner-scorecard]], the dangers of financial [[leverage]], and the necessity of extreme patience in allowing [[compounding]] to work. He also details how he identifies rare, extreme mispricings in global public markets.
