
In this video transcript, Brian Feroldi breaks down five rapid balance sheet rules of thumb attributed to Warren Buffett for assessing a company's financial strength. The rules evaluate a business's solvency, capital structure, and capital allocation habits by examining its cash relative to debt, debt-to-equity ratio, presence of preferred stock, trend in retained earnings, and presence of treasury stock. Feroldi applies these metrics to Chipotle (CMG) to demonstrate how to perform quick balance sheet checks and how to adjust standard financial ratios for accounting nuances like lease liabilities and share buybacks.
