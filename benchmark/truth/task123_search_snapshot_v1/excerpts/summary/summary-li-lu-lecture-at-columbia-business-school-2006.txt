
In this 2006 Columbia Business School guest lecture introduced by Bruce Greenwald, [[li-lu|Li Lu]] articulates the foundational mindset and practical analytical methods of [[value-investing|value investing]]. Drawing on Ben Graham's principles, he emphasizes viewing stock purchases as fractional business ownership, demanding a strict [[margin-of-safety|margin of safety]], and cultivating psychological traits suited to operating as a minority participant in a market dominated by short-term traders. Through detailed case studies of Timberland and Hyundai Department Store, [[li-lu|Li Lu]] demonstrates how exhaustive, primary-source research and continuous multidisciplinary study yield high-conviction insights, as discussed further in [[value-investing-as-owner-mindset-and-analytical-rigor]].
