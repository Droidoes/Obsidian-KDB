
In a 2013 panel discussion at Georgetown University moderated by CEO Brian Moynihan, Warren Buffett shares insights on his investing framework, economic views, and approach to philanthropy. He addresses core investment principles such as maintaining a [[circle-of-competence]], exploiting psychological market extremes during [[market-panics]], and seeking a [[margin-of-safety]]. He also details his philanthropic philosophy through the [[giving-pledge]], explaining why he distributes his wealth through major foundations rather than managing it directly.
