
A lecture delivered at the UC San Diego Stein Institute for Research on Aging reviewing the physiological functions of sleep and how sleep changes as humans age. The speaker details [[sleep-architecture]] and sleep stages, key restorative functions including [[memory-consolidation]], and core physiological drivers like the [[circadian-rhythm]]. It also reviews major age-associated sleep disorders—including [[obstructive-sleep-apnea]], [[insomnia]], [[advanced-sleep-phase-syndrome]], and [[rem-sleep-behavior-disorder]]—along with behavioral interventions like [[cognitive-behavioral-therapy-for-insomnia]] and [[sleep-hygiene]].
