
In this 2022 Boston College lecture, Mohnish Pabrai presents the [[uber-cannibals]] investing framework, explaining how companies that aggressively repurchase their own shares can deliver extraordinary long-term returns. Pabrai presents historical case studies including NVR, AutoZone, and Apple to demonstrate the power of share count reduction, while drawing heavily on the capital allocation playbook of [[henry-singleton]] at Teledyne. He emphasizes the critical precondition of business durability, warning against applying the cannibal framework to companies facing secular decline. The presentation concludes with a Q&A covering business valuation, circle of competence, philanthropy, and global equity markets.
