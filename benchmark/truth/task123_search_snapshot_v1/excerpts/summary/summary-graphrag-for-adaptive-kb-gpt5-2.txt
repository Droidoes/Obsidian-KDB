
This source describes the Synthetic Analyst Engine, a headless, AI-augmented research backend designed to compound qualitative investment insight over time. By transforming unstructured research into a [[knowledge-substrate]] comprising a relational [[knowledge-graph]] and vector memory, the system operationalizes qualitative analysis. Core architectural principles include [[delta-first-learning]] to track belief evolution, schema-constrained extraction with [[canonical-entity-resolution]], and explicit management of a [[circle-of-competence-exclusion-tracking|"Too Hard" knowledge base]]. The broader operational workflow is detailed in [[synthetic-analyst-engine-architecture]].
