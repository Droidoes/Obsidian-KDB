
In this interview on The Diary Of A CEO, Mohnish Pabrai outlines core frameworks for business and investing centered on risk minimization and asymmetric returns. He explains the [[dhandho-framework]] approach—entering businesses where the downside is negligible while the upside remains substantial—and highlights practices like [[cloning]], rapid prototyping based on customer feedback, and strict cost control. Pabrai also discusses personal productivity, the power of long-term [[compounding]], the [[rule-of-72]], the classification of human nature into [[givers-and-takers]], and the importance of holding onto high-performing assets.
