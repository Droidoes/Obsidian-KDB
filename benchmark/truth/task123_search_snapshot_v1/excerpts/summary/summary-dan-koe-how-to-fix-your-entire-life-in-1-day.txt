
Dan Koe outlines a comprehensive one-day protocol aimed at breaking habit loops and shifting personal identity to achieve long-term goal success. Drawing from psychology, [[cybernetics]], and ego development models, the essay details a structured daily exercise focusing on [[anti-vision]], pattern interrupts, and actionable goal setting. Koe synthesizes these principles into [[identity-based-behavior-change]], arguing that true transformation requires altering one's internal self-image and perceptual goals rather than relying on surface-level discipline.
