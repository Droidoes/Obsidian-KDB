
Dr. Michael Greger presents the core findings from his book 'How Not to Age,' focusing on evidence-based lifestyle interventions to slow aging. He discusses cellular pathways like [[autophagy]], the benefits of [[spermidine]], coffee, and [[whole-food-plant-based-diet|plant-based diets]] (notably [[legumes]], dark leafy greens, and cocoa), and offers practical advice on exercise, skin care, and preserving organ function.
