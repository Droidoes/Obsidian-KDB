
This chat log addresses the challenges of mapping heterogeneous financial report line items (such as Apple's 10-K) to normalized target schemas. It argues that exact lexical matching fails on financial statements because line items represent complex hierarchical compositions rather than flat synonyms. To solve this, the source proposes a [[financial-etl-decision-tree]] for ingestion, introduces specialized metrics like [[canonical-value-coverage]], and details the design of a [[canonical-financial-ontology]] that formalizes accounting rules, derived metrics like [[ontology-driven-ebitda]], and cross-statement reconciliation.
