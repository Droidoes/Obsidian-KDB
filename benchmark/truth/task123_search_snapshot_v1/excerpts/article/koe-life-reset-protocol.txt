
The One-Day Life Reset Protocol is a structured framework developed by Dan Koe to transition individuals through three distinct phases of transformation: dissonance, uncertainty, and discovery. By combining morning psychological excavation, scheduled pattern interrupts throughout the day, and evening synthesis, the protocol forces an identity shift. It utilizes the [[anti-vision]] to create necessary emotional tension, applies [[cybernetics]] through real-time feedback questions to break automatic behaviors, and grounds the resulting insights into actionable short-term targets to achieve [[identity-based-behavior-change]].
