
Following the May 2026 Beijing Summit, the strategic relationship between the US and China is characterized by asymmetric dependency across energy and critical minerals. The US attempt to build an independent supply chain via the [[myanmar-side-door]] reflects its vulnerability to [[rare-earth-supply-chains]] controlled by China. Conversely, while the US sought energy leverage against China through military action in [[operation-epic-fury]], the geopolitical reality in the [[strait-of-hormuz]] limits US intervention against Chinese oil supply. Because any direct interdiction of Chinese oil tankers risks an immediate weaponization of Chinese rare-earth export controls, Beijing holds effective deterrence against US naval escalation in the Persian Gulf.
