
Traditional medicine approaches aging piecemeal by treating individual diseases, but slowing the fundamental process of biological aging addresses multiple age-related chronic diseases simultaneously. Rather than relying on pharmaceuticals, core aging hallmarks can be modulated through lifestyle choices. Activating [[autophagy]] via moderate exercise, coffee, and [[spermidine]]-rich foods cleanses cellular debris. Concurrently, adopting a [[whole-food-plant-based-diet]] rich in [[legumes]], paired with targeted [[protein-restriction-longevity|protein and methionine restriction]], suppresses pro-aging pathways like TOR and IGF-1, offering a comprehensive strategy to extend healthspan and lifespan.
