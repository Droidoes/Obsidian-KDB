
The aphorisms of Napoleon Bonaparte synthesize a cohesive worldview centered on political and military pragmatism. At its core, this philosophy links [[human-motivation-and-levers]] directly to the mechanics of [[power-and-governance]]. Because people are predominantly driven by fear, self-interest, and vice, statecraft requires strategic adaptability and the careful cultivation of [[history-and-public-opinion]]. Furthermore, success in both warfare and politics depends on applying [[military-strategy-and-courage]]—exploiting adversary mistakes, managing perception, and converting individual ambition into collective power.
