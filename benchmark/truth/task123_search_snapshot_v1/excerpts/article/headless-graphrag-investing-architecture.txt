
Quantitative data in modern financial markets is rapidly priced in by algorithmic traders, leaving qualitative insights ('scuttlebutt') as a primary source of sustainable alpha. The 10x-Learning-Engine operationalizes this by building a headless backend that converts unstructured research newsletters into a structured [[knowledge-graph]] and vector space.

By leveraging [[graph-rag]], the architecture avoids the limitations of simple semantic retrieval by mapping complex structural relationships such as supply chain bottlenecks and competitive dynamics. Rather than continuously accumulating redundant commentary, the system applies a [[delta-protocol]]—comparing incoming information against vector baselines (`pgvector`) and existing graph edges to extract only net-new information deltas. Furthermore, by formalizing [[circle-of-competence-anti-patterns]], the engine systematically logs structural failure modes, providing an analytical terminal backend for deep value investing.
