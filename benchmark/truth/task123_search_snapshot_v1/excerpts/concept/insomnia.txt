
Insomnia involves chronic difficulty initiating sleep, maintaining sleep, or waking prematurely without the ability to fall back asleep, impairing daily functioning. According to the Spielman model, insomnia is driven by predispositions, precipitating stressors, and perpetuating behavioral factors (such as remaining in bed while awake or excessive clock-watching). Long-term clinical resolution relies on non-pharmacological behavioral reconditioning like [[cognitive-behavioral-therapy-for-insomnia]] rather than sedative medications.
