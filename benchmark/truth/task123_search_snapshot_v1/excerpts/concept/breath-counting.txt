
Breath Counting is a meditation practice rooted in traditional Zen traditions that centers awareness by anchoring attention to exhalations. The practitioner sits comfortably with a straight spine, closes their eyes, and allows the breath to flow naturally without forcing it. On each exhale, the practitioner mentally counts from one up to five, resetting back to one after reaching five. If the mind wanders or the count exceeds five, the practice directs a gentle, non-judgmental return to one. Daily practice for around 10 minutes helps focus the mind, reduce distractions, and cultivate a meditative state.
