
Rare earth supply chains rely heavily on raw heavy rare-earth ore mined in northern Myanmar (Kachin State) and transported across the border into Yunnan, China. Myanmar accounts for 60–70% of China's heavy rare earth imports, while China maintains an 80–90% monopoly on global processing and refining. Disruptions along these physical truck routes—caused by civil conflict, junta-KIA fighting, or border closures—directly constrain global supply and provide Beijing with immense leverage in trade negotiations.
