
Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I) is a structured non-pharmacological intervention targeting maladaptive behaviors and cognitive distortions surrounding sleep. Core techniques include stimulus control (associating the bed solely with sleep), sleep restriction (matching time in bed strictly to actual sleep duration to boost efficiency), and cognitive restructuring. CBT-I demonstrates equal short-term efficacy to pharmaceutical sleep aids (~50% reduction in sleep latency) and vastly superior long-term retention without risk of medication dependency.
