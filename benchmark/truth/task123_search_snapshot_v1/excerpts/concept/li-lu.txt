
Li Lu is a value investor, founder of Himalaya Capital, and graduate of Columbia University, where attending a Warren Buffett lecture inspired his investment career. His framework builds directly on Ben Graham and Warren Buffett, prioritizing an owner's mindset, exhaustive investigative research, and a strict [[margin-of-safety|margin of safety]]. [[li-lu|Li Lu]] emphasizes that successful [[value-investing|value investors]] represent a distinct minority (roughly 5% of assets) whose genetic or psychological disposition allows them to operate independently from the short-term trading majority.
