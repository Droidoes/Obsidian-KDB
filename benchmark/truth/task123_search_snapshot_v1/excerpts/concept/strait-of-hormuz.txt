
The Strait of Hormuz serves as a critical maritime chokepoint for global oil flows, including Iranian crude bound for China via shadow fleets and ship-to-ship transfers. While the US military enforces blockades against Iranian-flagged vessels, direct enforcement or interception against China-bound or Chinese-protected tankers carries prohibitive escalation risks. Any direct move against Chinese energy supply lines in the strait invites immediate retaliatory halts on Chinese rare-earth exports to the United States.
