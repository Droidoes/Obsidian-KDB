
Shareholders' equity represents the residual interest in a company's assets after deducting all liabilities, governed by the accounting equation: Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity. 

Key components within shareholders' equity include common stock, [[preferred-stock]], [[retained-earnings]], and [[treasury-stock]]. Tracking changes across these sub-accounts provides insight into how a firm funds its growth, reinvests profits, and executes shareholder return programs.
