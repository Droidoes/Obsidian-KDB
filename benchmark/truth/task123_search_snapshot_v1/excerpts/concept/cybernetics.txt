
Derived from the Greek *kybernetikos* ("to steer"), cybernetics is the study of goal-oriented, self-regulating systems. In the context of personal development and behavioral psychology, cybernetic intelligence is defined as the capacity to set a goal, act, sense feedback, compare current state to the target outcome, and iterate through trial and error. High-intelligence systems use continuous feedback loops to adapt, whereas low-intelligence systems fail by becoming stuck on obstacles without adjusting course.
