
Troubleshooting the Claude Desktop Cowork workspace on Windows 11 involves resolving service startup failures caused by MSIX package corruption, locked processes (`cowork-svc.exe`), and corrupted cache bundles. Complete remediation requires stopping locked processes, purging corrupted bundle directories in `%APPDATA%\Claude\vm_bundles` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\Claude*`, and restarting underlying virtualization components. Following a clean cache wipe, launching a new workspace triggers a fresh VM bundle download and re-initializes workspace integrations like Plan9/virtiofs mounts.
