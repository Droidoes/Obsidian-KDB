
Autophagy (derived from Greek for 'self-eating') is a cellular housekeeping and recycling mechanism where defective components and misfolded proteins are broken down and scrapped for spare parts. While autophagy naturally declines with age, leading to cellular waste accumulation, it can be stimulated naturally. Key triggers include aerobic exercise for at least 60 minutes, fasting, coffee consumption (both regular and decaf, via chlorogenic acid), and dietary intake of [[spermidine]].
