
The circadian rhythm is an endogenous daily biological cycle regulated primarily by the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) located in the anterior hypothalamus. Although individual body cells possess localized clocks, the SCN acts as the master pacemaker, operating on an intrinsic cycle of approximately 25 hours that is daily entrained to a 24-hour cycle by environmental light (zeitgebers). The circadian rhythm regulates alertness, hormone release (such as melatonin), and core body temperature, which dips to its lowest point in the early morning hours and peaks during wakefulness.
