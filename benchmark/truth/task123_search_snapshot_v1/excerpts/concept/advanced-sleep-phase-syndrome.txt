
Advanced Sleep Phase Syndrome is a circadian rhythm disorder common in older adults, characterized by a shift in the sleep schedule to significantly earlier hours (e.g., falling asleep at 7:00 PM and waking at 3:00 AM). It is caused by age-related attenuation of the suprachiasmatic nucleus, reduced endogenous melatonin secretion due to pineal gland calcification, and diminished exposure to morning sunlight (zeitgebers).
