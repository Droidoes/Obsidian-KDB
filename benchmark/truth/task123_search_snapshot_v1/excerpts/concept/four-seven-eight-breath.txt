
The 4-7-8 Breath, also known as the Relaxing Breath, is a structured breathing technique promoted by Dr. Andrew Weil to reduce stress and prepare the body for meditation or sleep. To perform the exercise, the tip of the tongue is placed against the tissue ridge behind the upper front teeth throughout the cycle. The practitioner exhales completely through the mouth with a whoosh sound, inhales quietly through the nose for a count of 4, holds the breath for a count of 7, and exhales completely through the mouth for a count of 8. Regular practice twice daily helps decrease anxiety and improve sleep quality.
