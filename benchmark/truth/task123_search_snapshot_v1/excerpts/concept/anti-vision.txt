
Anti-vision is a psychological tool defined by Dan Koe that involves explicitly detailing the exact future one fears and wants to avoid. By analyzing what life will look like in five to ten years if no behavioral adjustments are made, individuals generate the emotional dissonance and urgency required to break out of unconscious habit loops. Unlike standard positive visualization, the anti-vision leverages the natural aversion to undesirable outcomes as primary fuel for [[identity-based-behavior-change]].
