
The Myanmar Side Door is a proposed US diplomatic and supply-chain diversification route attempting to bypass Chinese rare-earth processing. The strategy seeks outreach to the Kachin Independence Organization (KIA) to divert raw rare-earth ore westward into India for processing under India's 2026 Rare Earth Mission before export to allied markets. Despite being a primary short-term hedge, the strategy faces severe feasibility constraints due to regional civil war, logistical barriers, sanctions compliance, and India's unproven pilot-stage processing capacity.
