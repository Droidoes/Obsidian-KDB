
The Dhandho framework is an approach to business and investing derived from the Gujarati business tradition that focuses on minimizing risk while preserving high upside potential—summarized by the adage "heads I win, tails I don't lose much." Rather than equating high return with high risk, a Dhandho operator uses creative deal structures, low overhead, and low capital requirements to eliminate downside risk. Notable examples include Sam Walton copying retail formats, Richard Branson leasing aircraft without upfront capital, and Patel motel operators eliminating payroll by using family labor.
